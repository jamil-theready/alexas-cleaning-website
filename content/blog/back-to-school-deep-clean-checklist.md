## Quick answer

**A back to school deep clean is a targeted reset of the four rooms where household illness actually spreads: the entry, the kitchen, the bathrooms, and the bedrooms.** It is not a spring clean. The goal is not sparkle, it is getting your home to a defensible baseline before thirty new sets of germs walk through your door every afternoon.

> **Quick verdict:** Start two weeks out, work in illness-risk order, and focus on high-touch surfaces over floors. As of August 2026, most El Dorado County families need two to four hours for a full reset.

## Why August beats September

The two weeks before the first day are the best cleaning window of the entire year, and most families miss it.

Once school starts, your weekends fill with practice schedules, homework, and the first round of colds. Cleaning stops being a project and becomes triage. Doing the reset in advance means you spend the school year maintaining a clean house instead of trying to dig one out.

There is a second reason. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, respiratory illness transmission climbs sharply once schools return to session, and high-touch surfaces are a documented transmission route. A house that starts the year disinfected is a house that starts the year ahead.

## The room by room checklist

Work in this order. It follows how germs actually enter and travel through a home, not how rooms are arranged.

**1. Entry and mudroom.** This is where the outside world lands. Wipe down door handles, light switches, and the backpack drop zone. Vacuum or wash entry mats. Clear out last year's shoes.

**2. Kitchen.** Disinfect the fridge handle, cabinet pulls, faucet, and the lunch prep counter. Empty and wipe the lunchbox shelf. Clean out the fridge before the first grocery run of the school year.

**3. Bathrooms.** Full disinfection of toilets, sinks, faucets, and every handle. Replace or launder hand towels. This is the single highest-value room in the whole checklist.

**4. Bedrooms.** Wash bedding including pillows and mattress protectors. Dust surfaces and vacuum under beds. Clear the desk so homework has somewhere to live on night one.

**5. Shared living space.** Vacuum upholstery, wipe remotes and game controllers, dust surfaces. Lower priority than the four above, and the first thing to cut if you run short on time.

## What actually matters versus what feels productive

| Task | Illness impact | Time it takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disinfecting handles and switches | High | 20 to 30 minutes |
| Bathroom deep clean | High | 30 to 45 minutes per bath |
| Washing all bedding | Medium | 2 to 3 loads |
| Mopping floors | Low | 30 to 60 minutes |
| Organizing closets | Very low | 2 to 4 hours |

The pattern is worth sitting with. The tasks that feel most productive, like reorganizing a closet, do the least for keeping your family healthy. The tasks that take twenty minutes and leave no visible trace do the most.

## The Two Week Reset

Here is the schedule we give families who want to do it themselves.

1. **Fourteen days out.** Declutter only. No cleaning yet. Clear surfaces so the actual clean has somewhere to happen.
2. **Ten days out.** Bedrooms. Bedding, dusting, under the beds, desks cleared.
3. **Seven days out.** Kitchen. Fridge, pantry, lunch station, all handles.
4. **Three days out.** Bathrooms. Full disinfection, fresh towels.
5. **Day before.** Entry and a fast pass on high-touch points everywhere.

Splitting it across two weeks turns a brutal weekend into five manageable sessions. If that still sounds like more than you have, that is exactly what a one-time [deep cleaning](/services/deep-cleaning) appointment is for.

## Where families in El Dorado County get stuck

We see the same two things every August.

The first is starting too late. A family decides on a Saturday that the house needs a reset, and school starts Monday. Everything gets a light pass and nothing gets a real one.

The second is scope creep. The deep clean turns into a garage reorganization, the garage turns into a donation run, and by Sunday night the bathrooms still have not been touched. Keep the checklist narrow. The closet can wait until October.

Families across [Placerville](/areas/placerville) and the surrounding foothills tend to book their August slots early, because the last two weeks of summer are the busiest cleaning window we have all year.

## Making it stick through the school year

A deep clean in August is worth very little by Halloween without some kind of maintenance behind it.

Most families we work with move to a [bi-weekly cleaning](/services/bi-weekly-cleaning) schedule once school starts. Every two weeks is frequent enough that the house never fully slides, and light enough that it does not feel like an extravagance. Families with younger kids or multiple pets often do better with [weekly service](/services/weekly-cleaning).

Whatever cadence you choose, set it up in August. Schedules fill through the fall, and the families who wait until they are desperate in October are the ones who cannot get the slot they want.

## Frequently asked questions

**When should I do a back to school deep clean?**
Two weeks before the first day of school is the ideal window. That gives you time to work room by room instead of cramming everything into one weekend, and it leaves room to reschedule if something comes up.

**What should I clean first for back to school?**
Start with the entry and mudroom, then the kitchen, then bathrooms, then bedrooms. This order follows how illness actually travels into a home, from shoes and backpacks inward to shared surfaces.

**How long does a back to school deep clean take?**
Most family homes need two to four hours for a genuine deep clean, depending on square footage and how long it has been since the last one. A professional team of two typically finishes a three bedroom home in about three hours.

**Is deep cleaning worth it before school starts?**
The first six weeks of school are peak season for household illness transmission. A deep clean that disinfects high-touch surfaces before that window starts is one of the few preventive steps that takes a single afternoon.
